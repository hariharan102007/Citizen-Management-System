# How to Run Citizen TN on Your iPhone (PWA Method)

You do **NOT** need a Mac, Xcode, or any App Store developer account. 

Follow either of these 2 quick free methods to get a live URL and install it on your iPhone home screen:

---

## 🚀 Method 1: Netlify Drop (Fastest — 30 Seconds, No Coding)

1. In Google AI Studio, click the top right menu and select **Export to ZIP**.
2. Unzip the downloaded file on your computer or phone.
3. On any browser, open:
   👉 **[https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)**
4. Drag and drop the `pwa` folder into the drop zone.
5. Netlify will immediately give you a free live **HTTPS link** (e.g. `https://citizen-tn-xxxx.netlify.app`).
6. **Open that link in Safari on your iPhone!**

---

## 🌐 Method 2: GitHub Pages (Free Permanent Hosting)

1. In AI Studio, click the menu and select **Push to GitHub**.
2. Open your repository on [github.com](https://github.com).
3. Go to **Settings > Pages**.
4. Under "Build and deployment", set:
   * **Source**: Deploy from a branch
   * **Branch**: `main` (or `master`)
   * **Folder**: `/ (root)` or `/pwa`
5. Click **Save**. Within 1 minute, GitHub provides your live link:
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/pwa`
6. **Open that link in Safari on your iPhone!**

---

## 📲 How to Add to iPhone Home Screen (Looks & Behaves Like a Real App)

1. Open the live link in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. At the bottom of Safari, tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up 📤).
3. Scroll down the menu and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (➕).
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
5. The **"Citizen TN"** icon now appears on your iPhone home screen!
6. When opened from your home screen, it runs full-screen (no Safari browser bars), accesses your iPhone's real GPS, and saves complaints offline.
